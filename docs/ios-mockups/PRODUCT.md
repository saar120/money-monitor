# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

ios

## Users

The maintainer and a small trusted circle of family and friends who use an individually approved iPhone with one Money Monitor Mac. They usually open the app for a short daily check, to find a transaction, review an AI suggestion, check a budget, or ask a focused question. They should not need to understand scraping, SQLite, or Tailscale. There are no cloud accounts, household roles, or multi-Mac profiles.

## Product Purpose

Give people a clear, trustworthy view of their Money Monitor data on iPhone while the Mac remains the only source of truth, credential holder, and scraper. The app connects privately to the Mac, caches the most recent read model for useful offline viewing, and makes the most common review tasks feel immediate and native.

Success means a person can understand their current financial position in seconds, find or review a transaction in under a minute, and always know whether the data is live or cached.

The self-use product ships through direct Xcode installation and private TestFlight. Replacement phones pair fresh and old devices are revoked from the Mac; credentials and cached financial data are not migrated phone-to-phone.

## Positioning

Your complete personal-finance picture on iPhone, without moving bank credentials or scraping into the cloud.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and private. Money Monitor should feel composed and quietly capable: more like a trusted system app than a trading terminal or a gamified budgeting product.

## Anti-references

- A desktop dashboard squeezed into a phone viewport.
- Neon crypto aesthetics, speculative language, or attention-grabbing market visuals.
- Corporate bank portals with dense forms, tiny labels, and unclear hierarchy.
- Decorative glass cards, excessive gradients, or a card around every piece of content.
- Sync language that exposes infrastructure details before the user needs them.

## Design Principles

1. **Glance first, details on demand.** Lead with the few numbers and decisions that matter today, then use native navigation for depth.
2. **Make local-first reassuring.** Show freshness and connection state in plain language without turning technical plumbing into the product.
3. **Review beats administration.** Optimize the phone for checking, confirming, searching, and understanding; keep rare configuration secondary.
4. **Native before novel.** Use iOS navigation, sheets, lists, controls, typography, and motion unless a departure provides clear user value.
5. **Explain the number.** Charts and totals always include comparison, period, or plain-language context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Bold Text, Increased Contrast, Reduce Transparency, and Reduce Motion. Every control has at least a 44 by 44 point target. Charts include a concise text summary and never rely on color alone. Positive and negative states combine icon, label, and color. Hebrew merchant names and mixed right-to-left content must remain legible inside an otherwise left-to-right interface.
