## Product-decision status

There are no unresolved product decisions blocking the current self-use roadmap. The detailed accepted baseline is [Locked product policy](LOCKED_PRODUCT_POLICY.md). Cloud, household roles, multi-Mac profiles, native push, App Store distribution, iPad optimization, full Hebrew UI localization, and offline mutations are explicitly out of scope rather than deferred questions.
