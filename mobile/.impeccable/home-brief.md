# Mobile product surface

Mode: Operate. The owner checks finances frequently and should understand the current month in seconds, clean transactions quickly, and explore causes without leaving the phone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm financial pulse that leads with comparison and attention, refusing the permanent-card dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: System background, SF typography, hairline structure, one deep-green tint, and color used only for financial meaning.

STORY: See current spending health, act on review work, then follow a change through category and merchant to transactions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Month and freshness sit quietly above one spending statement; income and pace explain it; a dual time/budget track and one contextual attention row complete the glance.

FORM: Native three-tab hierarchy with push drill-down and focused sheets; direct structure chosen from the settled product brief (no seed required).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
